How We Deliver

Four Delivery Models — Pick What Fits

Every partnership is delivered through one or more of these channels, depending on your needs and operations.

On-Site

We come to your facility with our own physiotherapists, equipment and protocols.

  • Dedicated room or department
  • Fixed weekly schedule
  • Fully managed service

Outsourced Department

We operate the physiotherapy function on your behalf — under your brand or ours.

  • Staffing & rota management
  • Clinical protocols & audit
  • Equipment procurement

Digital & Remote

Telerehab, digital exercise programmes and remote monitoring for distributed teams.

  • Video consultations
  • Digital exercise programmes
  • Progress dashboards

Mobile & Home Visits

We travel to your people — at home, in hotels, at work or in the field.

  • Home-based physiotherapy
  • Hotel & residential visits
  • Expat & relocation support
