Lymphatic Management Programmes

Specialist Service

Lymphatic Drainage & Management

We deliver lymphatic management programmes for institutions managing post-surgical, oncologic or chronic lymphoedema caseloads. Our physiotherapists are trained in advanced lymphatic techniques and can operate inside your facility, on a schedule you define.

  • Manual Lymphatic Drainage (MLD) — gentle, rhythmic techniques to stimulate lymph flow and reduce swelling.
  • Complete Decongestive Therapy (CDT) — combining MLD, compression therapy, exercise, and skin care.
  • Post-Surgical Lymphatic Care — reduce oedema and improve healing after orthopaedic, oncologic, or cosmetic procedures.
  • Lymphoedema Management — personalised programmes for primary and secondary lymphoedema.
  • Compression Garment Fitting & Education — guidance for ongoing self-management.
  • Exercise Prescription for Lymphatic Health — tailored movement programmes supporting lymphatic function.

Why It Matters for Partners

Lymphatic management is essential for patients with compromised lymphatic function. Our programme helps your organization:

  • Reduce patient pain and swelling
  • Improve joint mobility and function
  • Prevent complications post-surgery
  • Enhance tissue healing and recovery
  • Support long-term lymphoedema management
  • Improve quality of life and independence

Contact us to discuss how a lymphatic programme can be integrated into your service model.
