About Physio Dynamic Africa

An institutional physiotherapy partner — delivering evidence-based rehabilitation services to organizations across Mombasa, Nairobi and beyond.

Who We Are

A modern physiotherapy partner for Africa's institutions

Physio Dynamic Africa is a modern, innovative physiotherapy company headquartered in Mombasa, Kenya, with active service delivery in Nairobi and a growing pan-African partner network. We work with organizations — not just individuals — to design, deliver and manage physiotherapy and rehabilitation services for the people they are responsible for.

Our partners include corporates, hospitals, insurers and HMOs, schools and universities, sports organizations, NGOs, and expatriate and diplomatic missions. We embed licensed physiotherapists into their operations, deploy digital rehabilitation at scale, and take clinical accountability for outcomes.

Founded on clinical excellence, we blend evidence-based physiotherapy with technology-driven solutions — restoring mobility, preventing injury, and building long-term health capacity inside the organizations we serve.
