Executive Summary — Partnership Proposal

A structured collaboration to establish a state-of-the-art physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic at Bomu Hospital, delivering world-class rehabilitation care to patients across the Coast region and beyond.

Our Vision

Establish a state-of-the-art physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic within Bomu Hospital's premises — delivering world-class, evidence-based rehabilitation care to patients in Mombasa and across the Coast region.

Our Capabilities

  • Stroke recovery, orthopaedic and post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Sports injury management, geriatric and neurological rehabilitation
  • Lymphatic management, digital and telerehabilitation
  • Expert, evidence-based care delivered by licensed physiotherapists

Partnership Model

Bomu Hospital
