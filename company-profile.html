About Us

Physio Dynamic Africa is a modern, innovative physiotherapy company delivering services across Kenya, East Africa and the wider continent. We work with organizations — not just individuals — to design, deliver and manage physiotherapy and rehabilitation services for the people they are responsible for.

Our partners include corporates, hospitals, insurers and HMOs, schools and universities, sports organizations, NGOs, and expatriate and diplomatic missions. We embed licensed physiotherapists into their operations, deploy digital rehabilitation at scale, and take clinical accountability for outcomes — wherever our partners operate.

Founded on clinical excellence, we blend evidence-based physiotherapy with technology-driven solutions — restoring mobility, preventing injury, and building long-term health capacity inside the organizations we serve.
